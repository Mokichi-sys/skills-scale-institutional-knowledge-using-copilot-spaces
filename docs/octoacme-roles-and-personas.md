# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Coordinator

### Role Summary
Project Coordinators support Project Managers with administrative tasks, scheduling, and communication coordination. They enable efficient project operations by managing logistics, documentation, and stakeholder coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and coordinate project meetings, standups, and reviews
- Maintain project documentation and meeting notes
- Track action items and follow up on commitments
- Coordinate scheduling with multiple stakeholders
- Prepare and distribute project status reports and updates
- Manage project artifacts and version control

### Goals
- Ensure smooth project operations and logistics
- Improve project visibility through organized documentation
- Enable efficient communication and information flow across the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager**: Handles administrative and coordination tasks that allow Project Managers to focus on strategic planning and risk management
- **Supports Developers**: Manages meeting schedules and coordinates communication needs for development teams
- **Coordinates with Product Managers**: Facilitates alignment meetings and tracks decisions affecting project timelines
- **Engages all stakeholders**: Acts as a central hub for project information, meeting coordination, and status updates

### Typical Communication
- Meeting coordination emails and calendar management
- Project status reports and documentation summaries
- Action item tracking and follow-up reminders
- Administrative communication with team members and stakeholders

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads ensure quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle. They own testing strategy, quality planning, and acceptance criteria validation to deliver reliable software.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain quality assurance and testing strategies
- Plan testing activities across project phases
- Oversee testing execution and defect tracking
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before release
- Identify quality risks and recommend improvements
- Collaborate on test automation and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure products meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects reaching production environments
- Build confidence in software reliability and user experience

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Participates in design reviews, defines testable acceptance criteria, and coordinates testing efforts
- **Partners with Product Managers**: Validates features against acceptance criteria and communicates quality status for release decisions
- **Supports Project Managers**: Provides quality metrics and risk assessments for project planning
- **Works across the team**: Ensures quality considerations are integrated throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality strategy documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Acceptance criteria validation and sign-off
- Quality reviews during planning and retrospectives

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical guidance and design oversight throughout the project. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and align with organizational technical standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and guide technical architecture and design decisions
- Conduct technical design reviews and provide recommendations
- Assess technical risks and recommend mitigation strategies
- Evaluate technology stack and recommend tools and frameworks
- Ensure alignment with technical standards and best practices
- Mentor development teams on architectural patterns

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Enable knowledge sharing and development team growth

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, design patterns, and technical recommendations for implementation
- **Consults with Product Managers**: Discusses technical feasibility and implications of feature requirements
- **Supports Project Managers**: Identifies technical risks and dependencies that impact project planning
- **Partners with Quality Assurance**: Ensures technical design supports quality and testability goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and technical proposals
- Design review meetings and technical guidance sessions
- Risk assessments and technical trade-off analyses
- Technical documentation and pattern libraries

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the release and deployment process. They ensure smooth transitions from development to production while maintaining system stability and minimizing deployment risks.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and timelines
- Manage release documentation and change logs
- Coordinate deployment activities across teams
- Monitor deployment quality and post-deployment support
- Manage version control and release artifacts
- Communicate release status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Execute reliable and timely releases with minimal risk
- Ensure consistent communication about releases and deployment status
- Reduce deployment lead time while maintaining quality

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Developers**: Manages deployment activities and ensures development artifacts are ready for production
- **Partners with Project Managers**: Coordinates release timing with overall project schedules
- **Works with Quality Assurance**: Validates release readiness and coordinates post-deployment verification
- **Communicates with Operations**: Ensures infrastructure and systems are prepared for deployment
- **Updates Product Managers and stakeholders**: Provides release status and deployment impact information

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment schedules
- Change logs and deployment notes
- Release status updates and deployment notifications
- Post-deployment support coordination

---

## Compliance/Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance/Risk Officers oversee compliance and risk management activities throughout the project. They ensure projects meet regulatory requirements, organizational policies, and risk management standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct risk assessments and maintain risk registers
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational requirements
- Identify and document project risks and mitigation strategies
- Monitor risk exposure and escalate critical issues
- Review policies and ensure adherence to standards
- Support incident response and lessons learned processes

### Goals
- Ensure projects operate within acceptable risk parameters
- Maintain compliance with relevant regulations and standards
- Build organizational resilience through proactive risk management

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Supports Project Managers**: Helps identify, assess, and mitigate project risks; ensures proper escalation procedures
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Advises on technical risks and compliance considerations in implementation
- **Consults with Product Managers**: Reviews feature requirements for compliance and regulatory implications
- **Engages with Release Managers**: Ensures deployment processes comply with standards and risk policies
- **Works across all teams**: Provides guidance on compliance requirements and risk mitigation strategies

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Compliance checklists and audit documentation
- Risk mitigation plans and incident reports
- Escalation notifications and policy guidance

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded personas help simulate realistic project scenarios where multiple roles collaborate and bring different perspectives to problem-solving.
